Studying order

Ph.D. in Complex Systems (Universidad Adolfo Ibáñez). I research out-of-equilibrium dynamics, phase transitions, and self-organization using numerical simulation, statistical mechanics, and graph theory. Currently Associate Professor at Centro Multidisciplinario de Física, Universidad Mayor, Santiago.
